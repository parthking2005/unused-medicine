## Roles

### Overview
The project includes several roles, each with specific permissions and responsibilities.

### List of Roles
- **Admin**: Manages the overall system and has access to all functionalities.
- **Manager**: Oversees operations and manages resources.
- **Verifier**: Responsible for verifying information and actions.
- **Pickupman**: Handles pickups and deliveries.
- **Donator**: Users who donate items or resources.
